Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock — LeetCode 121 — one pass, O(1) space 👇

You have to buy before you sell. So the whole trick is: as you walk through the days, just remember the lowest price you've seen so far — that's the best day you could have bought. 🧠

Keep two values:
• minPrice — the cheapest day so far
• maxProfit — the best (today's price − minPrice) you've found

Each day: if today is a new low, update minPrice. Otherwise, check if selling today beats your best profit. That's it — one pass, no extra memory.

For [7,1,5,3,6,4]: buy at 1, sell at 6 → profit 5 ✅

→ O(n) time, O(1) space

Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (LeetCode 121) solved in one pass with O(1) space.

The key constraint is that you must buy before you sell. So as you scan the prices left to right, you keep track of the lowest price seen so far — the best day you could have bought — and the best profit found so far. On each day, if the price is a new minimum you update minPrice; otherwise you check whether selling today (price − minPrice) beats your running maxProfit. One pass through the array, no extra memory.

Example: [7,1,5,3,6,4] → buy at 1, sell at 6 → max profit = 5.
