Text Justification — LeetCode 68 — the spacing puzzle 👇

Pack words into lines of EXACTLY maxWidth characters, with both edges flush — like a newspaper column. It looks intimidating; it's really just three rules. 🧠

1) Greedily pack as many words as fit (word chars + the minimum one-space gaps ≤ maxWidth).
2) Spread the leftover spaces evenly across the gaps. If they don't divide evenly, the LEFT-hand gaps take the extra spaces.
3) The last line (or any single-word line) is left-justified: single spaces, then pad the right.

words = ["This","is","an","example","of","text","justification"], maxWidth = 16:
• "This    is    an"  → slack 8 over 2 gaps = 4 + 4
• "example  of text"  → slack 3 over 2 gaps = 2 + 1 (left gap extra)
• "justification   "  → last line, pad right

Every line is exactly 16 ✅

→ O(N) time over the total characters, O(1) extra space.

-- Title --
Text Justification — LeetCode 68 (Greedy Packing + Even Spacing)

-- Description --
Text Justification (LeetCode 68), the trickiest string problem in the set — broken into three simple rules.

The task: given a list of words and a maxWidth, format the text so each line is exactly maxWidth characters and fully justified (flush on both the left and right edges), except the last line.

Rule 1 — greedy packing: keep adding words to the current line while the word characters plus the minimum single-space gaps still fit within maxWidth. Rule 2 — space distribution: the leftover space on a line is slack = maxWidth − (total word characters). Spread it across the gaps between words; each gap gets slack / gaps spaces, and the first (slack % gaps) gaps get one extra — so the left-hand gaps are (at most one space) wider. Rule 3 — the last line, and any line with a single word, is left-justified: one space between words and the remaining width padded on the right.

Example with maxWidth = 16 and words ["This","is","an","example","of","text","justification"]:
• "This    is    an" — slack 8 across 2 gaps → 4 and 4 (even)
• "example  of text" — slack 3 across 2 gaps → 2 then 1 (left gap gets the extra)
• "justification   " — the last line, left-justified and right-padded

⏱️ O(N) time where N is the total number of characters, and O(1) extra space beyond the output.
