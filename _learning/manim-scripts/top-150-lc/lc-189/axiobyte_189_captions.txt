Rotate Array (LeetCode 189) solved in place with O(1) space using the reversal trick.

Rotating the array to the right by k means the last k elements wrap around to the front. Instead of copying into a new array, you can do it with three reversals: first reverse the entire array, then reverse the first k elements, then reverse the remaining n-k. The two sub-reversals restore the correct order within each part, and the result is exactly the rotated array — no extra space. Remember to do k %= n first in case k is larger than n.

Example: [1,2,3,4,5] with k=2 → reverse whole → [5,4,3,2,1] → reverse first 2 → [4,5,3,2,1] → reverse the rest → [4,5,1,2,3].

⏱️ O(n) time · O(1) space
