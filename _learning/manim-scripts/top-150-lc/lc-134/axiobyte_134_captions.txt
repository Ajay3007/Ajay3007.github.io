Gas Station — LeetCode 134 — greedy, O(1) space 👇

You're on a circular road of gas stations. gas[i] is the fuel you pick up at station i; cost[i] is the fuel it takes to reach the next one. Find a start you can loop all the way around from — or return -1.

Two ideas crack it 🧠
1) Feasibility: if total gas < total cost, no start works → -1.
2) The jump: walk left to right tracking a running tank. The moment the tank goes negative at station i, NO start from your current one up to i could have made it — so reset start to i+1 and tank to 0.

For gas = [1,2,3,4,5], cost = [3,4,5,1,2] the net per station is [-2,-2,-2,3,3]. The tank keeps dying through 0,1,2 (start slides to 3), then stays positive at 3 and 4. Total ends at 0 ≥ 0, so the answer is index 3 ✅

→ O(n) time, O(1) space — one pass, two counters.

-- Title --
Gas Station — LeetCode 134 (Greedy, O(1) Space)

-- Description --
Gas Station (LeetCode 134) solved with a greedy one-pass in O(1) space.

You're driving a circular route of gas stations. gas[i] is the fuel available at station i, and cost[i] is the fuel needed to drive from i to i+1. Return an index you can start at and complete the whole loop, or -1 if that's impossible (the answer is guaranteed unique when it exists).

Two observations do all the work. First, feasibility: if the total gas is less than the total cost, no starting point can work, so return -1. Second, the greedy jump: walk left to right keeping a running tank of gas minus cost since your current candidate start. The instant the tank goes negative at station i, every start from your current candidate up to i is disqualified — you couldn't have survived to i from any of them — so the next candidate must be i+1, and you reset the tank to 0.

Example: gas = [1,2,3,4,5], cost = [3,4,5,1,2] → net per station is [-2,-2,-2,3,3]. The tank dips negative at 0, 1, and 2, sliding the start to 3; from 3 onward the tank stays positive and the total finishes at 0. Since total ≥ 0, the answer is index 3.

⏱️ O(n) time · O(1) space
