-- Description --
Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock II (LeetCode 122) solved with a greedy one-pass in O(1) space.

Now you can complete as many transactions as you like, holding at most one share at a time. The key insight is that every bit of profit comes from the price going up, and the profit over any rising stretch equals the sum of its consecutive daily increases. So instead of hunting for the best buy and sell days, you simply add every positive step: whenever prices[i] > prices[i-1], add the difference to your total. That captures buying at every valley and selling at every peak automatically.

Example: [7,1,5,3,6,4] → +4 (1 to 5) and +3 (3 to 6) = 7. (With only one transaction allowed, the same prices give 5.)

⏱️ O(n) time · O(1) space
